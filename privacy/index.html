Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

What we collect

  • Tool inputs (numbers, names, dates) are processed in your browser and not sent to us, unless you submit them in a form.
  • Form submissions (name, email, message, numbers) are delivered to our inbox through the form service FormSubmit and used only to answer you.
  • Cookies and analytics: only with your consent. We may use Google Analytics and Google AdSense.

Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Partners

If you tick the partner box on our report form, we may share your request with one vetted partner (for example a Mandarin tutor) to answer it. We never sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time via the contact form. Under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws, you also have the right to object and to complain to your local regulator.

Retention

We keep form messages for up to 24 months, then delete them. Newsletter subscribers may unsubscribe at any time.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
