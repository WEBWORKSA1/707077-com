How the score is calculated

  • Digit weights: 8 (+), 6 and 9 (+), 2 and 3 (slight +), 4 (strong −).
  • Lucky combinations: 168, 518, 88, 888, 666, 99, 1314, 520 and 28 add bonus points.
  • Unlucky combinations: 14, 514, 74, 748, 250 and 44 subtract points.
  • The ending: finishing on 8 is prized; finishing on 4 is avoided.
  • Patterns: repeated digits (888) and rising runs (步步高, “step by step higher”) look premium.

Why it matters: research on Hong Kong plate auctions found one extra 8 lifts the expected price by about 65%. Agents, carriers and dealers price numbers with this logic.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a phone number lucky in China?

Many 8s (prosperity), 6s (smoothness) and 9s (longevity), ending in 8, and no 4s. Combinations like 168 (fortune all the way) and 518 (I will prosper) are especially valued.

Which licence plates are luckiest?

Short plates and plates full of 8s, 6s and 9s. In Hong Kong, plates such as 28, 18 and 9 have sold for millions of Hong Kong dollars.

Should I avoid the number 4?

Many Chinese buyers do. Some buildings skip 4th, 14th and 24th floors. If you sell to Chinese-speaking customers, avoiding 4 in prices and numbers is common practice.
