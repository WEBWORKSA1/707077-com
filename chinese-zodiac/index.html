Frequently asked questions

What Chinese zodiac year is 2027?

2027 is the Year of the Goat (羊), beginning on Chinese New Year, 6 February 2027. 2026 is the Year of the Horse, which began on 17 February 2026.

Why does my zodiac differ from the year I was born?

The zodiac year starts on Chinese New Year, which falls between 21 January and 20 February. If you were born before that date, you belong to the previous year's animal.

Which signs are most compatible?

Tradition favours the harmony trines — Rat-Dragon-Monkey, Ox-Snake-Rooster, Tiger-Horse-Dog, Rabbit-Goat-Pig — and the six “secret friend” pairs such as Rat-Ox and Horse-Goat.
