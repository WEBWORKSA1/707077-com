Frequently asked questions

Why do Chinese people use numbers as slang?

Typing digits was fast on early pagers and phones, and Chinese has many near-homophones. Codes like 520 (I love you) and 886 (bye-bye) became shorthand that is still widely used.

What does 233 mean?

Laughter, like LOL. It comes from emoticon number 233 on an early Chinese forum, which showed a figure pounding the floor with laughter.

What does 666 mean in Chinese?

“Awesome” or “so skilful”. 六 (liù) sounds like 溜 (liū), meaning smooth or slick. It is common in gaming and live-stream comments.
