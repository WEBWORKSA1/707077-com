Frequently asked questions

What does 707077 mean?

Using the same sound logic as classic Chinese pager codes (7 = 亲 qīn, “kiss/dear”; 0 = 你 nǐ, “you”), 707077 reads as 亲你亲你亲亲 — “kiss you, kiss you, kiss-kiss”. 77 also evokes 七夕 (Qixi), the 7th day of the 7th lunar month, Chinese Valentine's Day. It is a playful reading, not an official term.

Is 7 a lucky number in China?

Mixed. 七 (qī) echoes 起 (rise), 气 (energy), 齐 (together) and 亲 (kiss), and it anchors Qixi, the festival of lovers. But the 7th lunar month is also Ghost Month, so many people treat 7 as neutral rather than lucky.

What is the luckiest number in Chinese?

8 (八 bā), because it sounds like 发 (fā, to prosper). Combinations such as 88, 168 and 888 are prized for phone numbers, licence plates and prices.
