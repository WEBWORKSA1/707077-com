Frequently asked questions

How does the decoder work?

It splits your number into the longest known slang codes (like 520 or 1314) and reads any leftover digits by their sound. It then scores the number with weights drawn from common Chinese number beliefs: 8, 6 and 9 add points; 4 and combinations like 14 or 748 subtract.

Is the luck score scientific?

No. It is a cultural reading for education and fun. It reflects how many Chinese-speaking buyers perceive a number, which can affect prices for plates and phone numbers.

Does it work for Cantonese?

It uses Mandarin sounds, with Cantonese notes where they matter. A few readings (like 28 = easy fortune) come from Cantonese.
