What Does 707077 Mean? Reading a Chinese Love Code

By 707077 Editorial · Updated 1 October 2026

Short answer: 707077 reads as 亲你亲你亲亲 (qīn nǐ, qīn nǐ, qīn qīn) — “kiss you, kiss you, kiss-kiss”. It is a playful love code built on the same rules as 520 (“I love you”).

How Chinese number codes work

Mandarin has relatively few syllables, so many words sound alike. In the pager era of the 1990s, people sent messages using only digits, and each digit stood in for a word with a similar sound. 5 (wǔ) became 我 (wǒ, I), 2 (èr) became 爱 (ài, love) and 0 (líng) became 你 (nǐ, you). So 520 = 我爱你, “I love you”. The habit survived into texting, social media and red-packet amounts.

Why 7 means “kiss”

七 (qī) is close to 亲 (qīn), which means “kiss” or “dear”. Classic pager-code lists include 770 (亲亲你, kiss you), 775 (亲亲我, kiss me) and 721 (亲爱的, darling). 7 also echoes 起 (rise), 气 (vital energy) and 齐 (together).

Why 0 means “you”

In number codes, 0 regularly stands for 你 (you): 04551 is 你是我唯一, “you are my one and only”, and 0594184 is 你我就是一辈子, “you and me, for life”.

77 and Qixi

Repeat 7 and you get 七七, which points to the 7th day of the 7th lunar month: Qixi, Chinese Valentine's Day. That gives 707077 a double meaning: a string of kisses ending on the festival of lovers.

How to use 707077

  • As a sign-off to a partner: “707077 ❤”.
  • As a red-packet amount on love days (70.70 or 707.07 in your currency).
  • Combined: 707077 + 1314 = kisses forever.

A note on 77: 七七 is also the Chinese name for the Marco Polo Bridge Incident of 7 July 1937 (七七事变), a solemn anniversary. Context makes the romantic meaning clear, but avoid using “77” playfully on 7 July in Chinese-speaking contexts.

Frequently asked questions

Is 707077 an official Chinese slang term?

No. It is a reading built from well-established code conventions (7 = 亲, 0 = 你). Native speakers will recognise the logic, especially alongside 770 and 520.

Is 707077 lucky?

It scores as neutral-to-mixed on wealth (no 8s) but strong on love, thanks to its 7s and the Qixi link. It contains no 4.
