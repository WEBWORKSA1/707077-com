How to Choose a Lucky Phone Number (Chinese Method)

By 707077 Editorial · Updated 1 October 2026

A good number is easy to remember, sounds lucky, and holds its value. Use this five-step method — the same logic dealers use to price numbers.

  1. Remove the 4s. Each 4 drags a number down, especially at the end.
  2. Finish strong. Endings in 8 (prosper), 9 (lasting) or 6 (smooth) are the most valued. Endings like 168, 518 or 888 are premium.
  3. Look for words, not just digits. 1314 (lifetime), 520 (love) and 3344 (forever) add meaning; avoid 14, 74, 748 and 250.
  4. Prefer patterns. Repeats (AAA, ABAB) and rising runs (步步高) are easier to remember and resell.
  5. Match the purpose. A business hotline should shout prosperity (8s); a personal or couple's number can carry love codes (520, 707077).

Is a premium number worth paying for?

If you deal with Chinese-speaking clients, yes, within reason: the number is part of your brand. Research on Hong Kong plates shows each 8 can lift value by about 65%. For a personal number, a free “good” number from your carrier's pool is usually enough.
