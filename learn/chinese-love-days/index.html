Chinese Love Days: 520, 521, Qixi and More

By 707077 Editorial · Updated 1 October 2026

China doesn't have one Valentine's Day — it has several. Each has its own date, numbers and gift habits.

DayDateWhy
情人节14 FebruaryWestern Valentine's Day, widely celebrated in cities.
52020 May5-2-0 sounds like 我爱你, “I love you”. Peak day for confessions, weddings and 520-yuan red packets.
52121 May我愿意, “I'm willing” — the reply to 520.
七夕 Qixi7th day, 7th lunar monthThe traditional festival of lovers: 19 Aug 2026, 8 Aug 2027.
White Day14 MarchReturn-gift day, popular with younger couples.

520: the internet's Valentine's Day

WeChat red packets are normally capped at 200 yuan, but the platform has allowed 520-yuan packets on love days such as 20 May, and many couples choose the date to register their marriage. Marketers treat it as a second Valentine's Day.

Qixi: the classic

Qixi honours the cowherd Niulang and the weaver Zhinü, who meet once a year on a bridge of magpies. See our Qixi guide for dates, greetings and gifts.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 20 May a love day in China?

Because 5-2-0 sounds like 我爱你 (wǒ ài nǐ), “I love you”.

When is Chinese Valentine's Day?

Traditionally on Qixi, the 7th day of the 7th lunar month: 19 August 2026 and 8 August 2027.
