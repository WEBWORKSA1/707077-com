What does 8 mean in Chinese?

8 means “Prosperity — the luckiest number”. It sounds like 八 → 发 (bā → fā).

8 sounds like 发, to prosper. The Beijing Olympics opened at 8 pm on 8 August 2008, and one more 8 can lift a Hong Kong plate's price by about 65%.
