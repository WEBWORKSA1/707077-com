What does 707077 mean in Chinese?

707077 means “Kiss you, kiss you, kiss-kiss”. It sounds like 亲你亲你亲亲 (qīn nǐ qīn nǐ qīn qīn).

Built from two classic conventions — 7 for 亲 (kiss) and 0 for 你 (you) — and ending on 77, the Qixi date. A playful, affectionate sign-off rather than an official term. It is also the name of this site.
