What does 1314 mean in Chinese?

1314 means “For a lifetime / forever”. It sounds like 一生一世 (yīshēng yīshì).

Sounds like “one life, one world”. Popular in wedding gifts, red-packet amounts (13.14, 1314) and engraved jewellery.
