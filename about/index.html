About 707077

In Chinese, numbers talk. 8 promises fortune, 4 whispers death, 520 says “I love you”. Our name, 707077, reads as 亲你亲你亲亲 — “kiss you, kiss you, kiss-kiss” — and 77 points to Qixi, Chinese Valentine's Day.

We built 707077 to make that hidden language usable for everyone: people choosing a phone number, couples planning a date, learners decoding chat slang, and businesses pricing for Chinese-speaking customers.

What we believe

  • Useful first. Every page answers a real question fast.
  • Accurate and honest. We separate tradition from superstition and cite real data. Number meanings are cultural, not predictive.
  • Free for everyone. Ads, sponsors and supporters keep the tools free.
  • Respectful. We avoid stereotypes and flag regional differences.
