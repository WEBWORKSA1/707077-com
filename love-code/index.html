Frequently asked questions

How do I say I love you in numbers in Chinese?

520 (wǔ èr líng) sounds like 我爱你 (wǒ ài nǐ). Add 1314 (一生一世, a lifetime) to make 5201314: “I love you forever”.

Is the love calculator real?

It is for fun. It turns both names into a stable number, so the same pair always gets the same result.

Can I send the card?

Yes. Press “Send this code” to share a link. Whoever opens it sees the card with your code and its meaning.
