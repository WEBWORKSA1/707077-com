The legend of the cowherd and the weaver girl

Niulang (牛郎), a poor cowherd, falls in love with Zhinü (织女), a celestial weaver. They marry and have two children. When the gods discover the match, the Queen Mother of the West draws a river across the sky — the Milky Way — to separate them.

Moved by their love, magpies fly together once a year to form a bridge (鹊桥) so the couple can meet on the seventh night of the seventh month. In the sky they are the stars Altair and Vega, either side of the Milky Way.

Traditions

  • Young women once prayed to Zhinü for skill, threading needles by moonlight.
  • Qiaoguo (巧果), fried pastries of flour, oil and honey, date back to the Song dynasty.
  • Today couples swap flowers, jewellery and dinners, and send 520 or 1314 red packets.

Frequently asked questions

When is Qixi Festival 2027?

Sunday, 8 August 2027. In 2026 it fell on Wednesday, 19 August; in 2028 it falls on Saturday, 26 August.

Is Qixi the same as Tanabata?

They share the same legend and origin. Japan's Tanabata is usually held on 7 July in the Gregorian calendar, while Qixi follows the lunar calendar.

What does 77 mean in Chinese?

七七 points to the 7th day of the 7th month, which is Qixi. In chat slang, 77 can also read as 亲亲 (qīnqīn), “kiss-kiss”.
